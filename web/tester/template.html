How to use this keyboard tester

Choose a layout and press keys. Tab tests the Tab key. Shift+Tab stops testing and returns to page navigation. Click the keyboard, or focus it and press Enter or Space, to resume. Reset test clears results and resumes testing.
Fn and system shortcuts may not reach the browser.
